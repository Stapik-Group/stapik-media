# Overview

Stapik Media keeps track of what you have watched, read, listened to and played.

The sidebar on the left lists the categories: movies, series, cartoons, books, albums and games. Choose a category to see its entries, newest consumption dates included. The **Add** button below the list creates a new entry in the selected category.

Everything is saved on your computer after every change. The program remembers the last selected category, the language, the theme and the size of the window.
