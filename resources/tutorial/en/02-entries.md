# Entries

## Adding an entry

Press **Add**. The title is required: if you leave it empty, the program asks you to fill it in. The other fields depend on the category:

- movies, series and cartoons: director and genre,
- books: author, genre and whether it is an audiobook,
- albums: performer, publisher and genre,
- games: studio, publisher and platform.

**Release date** can be as exact as you know it. Choose the precision (day, month or year) and the fields that do not matter are hidden.

**When watched** is the month and year you saw the entry. The label follows the category: *When read* for books, *When listened to* for albums and *When completed* for games. The list uses the same words, for example *Read: March 2026*. For a new entry it starts as the current month.

**Suggestions.** While you type a director, author, performer, studio, publisher, genre or platform, the program offers values you have already entered for the same kind of entries (movies, series and cartoons share theirs), the most used first. Pick one to avoid typing it again and keep the spelling the same.

## Rating and note

Both are optional. **Rating** is a number from 1 to 10; choose *No rating* to leave it empty. **Note** is free text of any length, for example what you thought or who recommended it. The rating is shown in the list next to the dates, the first line of the note under them (hover over it to read all of it), and both are included in the CSV export and import and in the printed list (the rating only).

## One entry per title

The same title released in the same year can appear only once in a category. If you add an entry, or change one, so that it matches another one in its category, title and year of the release date, the program tells you so and keeps the window open, so that nothing you typed is lost. Letter case and spaces around the title do not matter, and neither does how exact the release date is. A remake with the same title but another release year is a separate entry, and so is the same title in a different category (a book and its film, say). To come back to something you have already listed, edit its entry.

Only the entries loaded on this device are compared, so an entry of an earlier year that is kept only in the cloud and has not been downloaded yet is not taken into account.

## Editing and deleting

Every entry has **Edit** and **Delete** buttons. Both can be undone, see [Undo and backups](#undo-and-backups).
